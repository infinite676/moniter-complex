Network Troubleshooter & Repair Actions

Flush & Clear DNS Cache

Clears stale hostname records and fixes 'Domain Not Found' or redirected sites.

ipconfig /flushdns
🔄

Release & Renew DHCP Lease

Forces your router to assign a fresh IP address, resolving LAN IP conflicts.

ipconfig /release && ipconfig /renew
🛡️

Reset TCP/IP & Winsock Stack

Repairs corrupted network socket bindings and restores broken connections.

netsh winsock reset && netsh int ip reset
